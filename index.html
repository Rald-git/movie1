The Green Mile

My Favorite Movie

Introduction

The Green Mile is one of my favorite movies because it is emotional, meaningful, and has an interesting story. The movie shows the lives of prison guards and prisoners on death row.

Plot

The story follows Paul Edgecomb, a prison guard who works on death row. He meets John Coffey, a mysterious prisoner with special abilities. As Paul learns more about John, he begins to question what is right and wrong.

Cast

  • The Green Mile features several talented actors:
  • Tom Hanks plays Paul Edgecomb
  • Michael Clarke Duncan playsJohn Coffey
  • Other actors include David Morse and Sam Rockwell

    • My Review

    I like The Green Mile because it has a powerful story and emotional moments. I also like the characters because they show kindness, compassion, and the importance of treating others fairly.
